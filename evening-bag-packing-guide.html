The Evening Bag Packing Guide

The beauty of an evening bag is how little it holds. Packing well means bringing only what you need, and knowing where else to keep the rest.

IThe core five

  1. Phone
  2. A slim cardholder with one card and ID
  3. Keys, ideally on a small ring
  4. Lipstick or balm
  5. A folded tissue

Try the what-fits tool before you buy.

IIShrink everything

Swap your wallet for a cardholder, take one key off the ring, decant hand cream into a tiny pot and carry blotting papers instead of a full compact. Small habits free up surprising space.

IIIPhone first

Large phones are the most common reason a clutch will not close. Measure your phone and check the inside dimensions of the bag, not the outside, before buying.

IVStay hands-free

Use the hidden chain, choose a wristlet, or keep the clutch tucked under your arm while you eat. At a seated dinner, the clutch can sit on your lap or by your side, not on the table.

VPlan the overflow

Coat pockets, a partner’s pocket or the cloakroom can carry what the clutch cannot. A tiny folding tote in your coat is useful for gifts or favours on the way home.
