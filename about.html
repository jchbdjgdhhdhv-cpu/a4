✦ About Clutchy ✦

For the small bag that finishes the look.

Clutchy is an independent website about clutches and evening bags.

We love the little bag that comes out for the big nights. Our guides explain the shapes, materials and tricks that make one work, and our tools help you match a clutch to an occasion and pack it well.

We do not sell bags, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
