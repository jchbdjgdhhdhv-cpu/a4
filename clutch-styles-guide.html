The Clutch Styles Guide

A clutch is the simplest bag there is, and the easiest to get wrong. Here is how shape, material and closure change the look and what it can carry.

IStart with the shape

Envelope clutches are the most versatile, from daytime events to dinners. Frame and box clutches feel more formal and hold their shape. Minaudières are small works of art for the grandest nights. Soft pouches are relaxed and roomy.

IIPick a material

MaterialFeelBest for
SatinSoft sheen, formalBlack tie, weddings
VelvetRich, autumn and winterDinners, holidays
Smooth leatherClean, lasts yearsWork events, dinners
MetallicWorks as jewelleryParties, galas
Beaded or sequinnedStatementSimple outfits that need sparkle
Raffia or strawLight, summeryGarden parties, holidays

IIIClosures

Magnetic snaps are quick but less secure; zips and kiss-locks keep things in. Box clasps feel satisfying and protect contents. For crowded events, choose a closure you can open with one hand.

IVStraps and chains

Many clutches hide a slim chain inside. It turns the bag into a shoulder bag when you need both hands, for dancing, eating or greeting. Tuck it inside when you want the clean clutch look.

VColour strategy

A metallic in gold or silver, matched to your jewellery, goes with almost everything. Black and nude are safe bets. A bold colour or a sparkling clutch is a great way to lift a simple dress.
